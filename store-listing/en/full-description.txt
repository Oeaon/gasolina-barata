Find the cheapest petrol station near you and see how much you save when you fill up. Gasolina Barata uses the official prices published by the Spanish Ministry for the Ecological Transition, updated every 30 minutes.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See the petrol stations around you sorted by price or distance.
• Choose your fuel: Petrol 95, Petrol 98, Diesel, Premium diesel or LPG.
• Search within 5, 10 or 25 km.
• See the average price in your area and the cheapest station.
• Find out how much you save filling your tank at each station compared with the average.
• Check each station's address, opening hours and distance.
• Get directions with one tap.
• Use it offline with the last list downloaded.

YOUR LOCATION NEVER LEAVES YOUR PHONE
The app downloads the full list of petrol stations in Spain and works out distances on your phone. Your location is never sent to any server or stored.

HOW MUCH WILL THE TRIP COST?
The trip calculator tells you how many litres you need for a journey based on your car's consumption and what it costs filling up at the cheapest station or at the average price.

PREMIUM
Premium removes all ads and keeps the trip calculator always unlocked. Choose a monthly subscription or a one-off lifetime payment. You can also unlock the calculator for free for 24 hours by watching an ad.

DATA
Official prices including taxes, from the public service of the Spanish Ministry for the Ecological Transition. The pump price always prevails.

Official data source:
• Ministry Petrol Station Geoportal: https://geoportalgasolineras.es/geoportal-instalaciones/Inicio
• Fuel price service: https://sedeaplicaciones.minetur.gob.es/ServiciosRESTCarburantes/PreciosCarburantes/help
